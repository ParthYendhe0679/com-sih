   Accused Rajesh Gaikwad was found actively operating dye-sublimation PVC card printers, embossers, and unauthorized software generating fabricated Aadhaar and PAN cards.

   High-end computing hardware, 320 blank PVC smartcards, and rubber stamps of various commercial institutions were seized under panchnama. Accused Rajesh Gaikwad has been arrested and remanded into police custody for custodial interrogation.
